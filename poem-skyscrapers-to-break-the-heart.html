Skyscrapers To Break The Heart

Skyscrapers all the way
Skyscrapers to break the heart
Rockhounds slowly melt the face
Accidental God of Art
Born from Subterranea
Quantum grace
Until it buckles.
Nine hundred years ago we had glaciers
And now rubble
