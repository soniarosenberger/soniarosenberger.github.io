T Minus Five to Sixty

I might forget about you
If you make me wait five minutes
Known for my impatience
Fast-moving restless child on vacation
This year will not be wasted
The last is growing ancient, by the day
Since I finished changing
I’m a sinner one Sunday from salvation
So don’t be late, I’m gonna move on

I’ll do it, I’ll be gone, I’ll forget about you
If I have to wait five more minutes

My path never changed when
I’d resolved my destination
You were sleeping at the station
You need to get up, don’t keep me waiting
Or else awaken
Alone
On the cold and sleep-forsaken pavement
