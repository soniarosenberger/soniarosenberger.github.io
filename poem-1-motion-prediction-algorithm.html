#1 Motion Prediction Algorithm

Lost my flashlight, lost my pen
Leaving behind everything important to me it seems
Will we be friends again?
I wished you hadn’t met people sometimes

Because I’m evil or something
(I must be)

I thought you might want to keep knowing me.
I don’t really have several hundred hours
Would’ve made a better bet on something else
Could have predicted more carefully
But why would I do that?

I wish you told me where your money was, because then I wouldn’t have had to watch you sell this stuff.
Saved up for a while and then
thrown out for the sake of a grudge

It was important to me
So I said I was sorry and held my hand out
Informing those watching
that they’d be able to win something here

It doesn’t feel like it was exactly my responsibility
But nonetheless
I must not let it bother me too much
