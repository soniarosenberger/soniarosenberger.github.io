You Look Like A Butterfly

You look like a BUTTERFLY!!!
I FEEL LIKE A DRAGONFLY :)
Dripped out, underwater! Glass! Drenched silver!
My dress is painted on!!! I look like a LIGHTNING STRIKE!!!
Get this MISERABLE LOSER out of my
W H I M S I C A L  C A N D Y  C A N E  L I F E
In the midst of a butterfly fling
This handsome beauty
Soft-to-the-touch
(indulging while wary)
(a kiss doesn’t scare me)
But I’ve already gone too far, because LOOK:
I deliver him
a single touch
And there he goes, from THAT THERE cliff!
HEAD OVER HEELS
It’s familiar but this BAFFLES ME
